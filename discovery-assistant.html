CONTROLLED EVALUATION · 3 INDEPENDENT RUNS

Strong evidence tracing. Incomplete theme coverage.

The same 40-record synthetic dataset, model and prompt were used for each run. Every generated theme was mapped to a documented human reference before scoring.

100% citation validity Every cited feedback ID existed in the source dataset.
95.5% mean evidence precision Citations usually supported the theme they were attached to.
66.7% mean theme coverage Two of the three human-reference themes were found consistently.
What the model missed

All three runs found the two dominant operational problems but missed the smaller payment communication and audit-history theme. Relevant records were often retrieved but grouped into a broader payment-status theme.

Product decision

Treat theme separation as the next experiment and keep RAG deferred. The evidence points to a grouping and coverage problem—not a failure to retrieve the source records.

Review every run and limitation ↗
Scope and methodology

This is a directional benchmark using one 40-record synthetic dataset, one model and one fixed prompt—not evidence of performance on larger or real-world datasets. “Independent runs” means three separate API calls using identical recorded inputs; no result was excluded.

AI-assisted development helped implement the prototype and draft supporting artefacts. I retained responsibility for the product framing, reviewed the theme mappings, made the evaluation decisions and approved the published conclusions.

STEP 2 · REVIEW FINDINGS

Inspect the evidence before accepting the interpretation.

AI findings are proposals. Expand the cited evidence, edit the interpretation and record a human decision.

Keep the human decision

Exporting the review preserves the source finding, any edits, the decision and the reviewer’s note.

WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES

AI assists. Product judgement decides.

The underlying prototype can analyse an uploaded CSV with an LLM. The public version focuses on the higher-value product question: can a reviewer trace, challenge and govern the output?
