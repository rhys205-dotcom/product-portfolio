# Product Portfolio

Source for [dowsall.com](https://dowsall.com), my personal product portfolio.

The site presents selected work across payments, regulated financial services, SaaS and applied AI. It is designed to show how I approach complex product problems: understand the evidence, make trade-offs explicit, deliver practical change and measure the outcome.

## Selected themes

- Scaling digital payments and improving financial workflows
- Delivering regulated digital banking journeys, including PSD2 Strong Customer Authentication
- Applying AI to product discovery with traceability and human oversight
- Translating customer, operational and regulatory complexity into clear product decisions

## Related project

The portfolio links to my [AI Product Discovery Assistant](https://github.com/rhys205-dotcom/ai-product-discovery-assistant), an experiment for turning unstructured customer feedback into evidence-backed themes and opportunities.

## Technology

The site is intentionally lightweight:

- Semantic HTML
- Responsive CSS
- No framework or runtime dependencies
- Hosted on Cloudflare Pages

This keeps the portfolio fast, accessible and straightforward to maintain.

## Development approach

The product direction, examples and editorial decisions are my own. AI-assisted tools have supported implementation, review and iteration.
